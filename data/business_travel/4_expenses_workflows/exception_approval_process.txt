VERITAS CORPORATE - EXCEPTION APPROVAL PROCESS 2026

1. VALID GROUNDS FOR EXCEPTIONS
Standard T&E limits may only be bypassed under the following specific circumstances:
- Major City Events: If a city is hosting a tier-1 global event (e.g., Mobile World Congress, Web Summit, Olympics) that artificially inflates all hotel prices above the corporate cap.
- Last-Minute Emergency Travel: If a client emergency requires travel within 48 hours and no economy flights are available within the budget.
- Security Mandates: If the Corporate Security team mandates a specific, more expensive hotel or transport method due to localized risks.

2. THE APPROVAL CHAIN (PRE-TRIP ONLY)
- All exceptions MUST be requested and approved BEFORE the booking is confirmed or the expense is incurred.
- The employee must submit the itinerary and the cost difference to their Direct Manager.
- If the overage is greater than 20% of the standard policy limit, it requires secondary approval from the VP of Finance.

3. UNAUTHORIZED OVERSPEND
If an employee books an over-budget flight or hotel without pre-approval, the Finance Department will ONLY reimburse up to the maximum policy limit. The remaining balance will be automatically deducted from the employee's next payroll.
