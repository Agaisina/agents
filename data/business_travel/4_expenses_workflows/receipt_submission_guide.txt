VERITAS CORPORATE - RECEIPT SUBMISSION & REIMBURSEMENT GUIDE

1. SUBMISSION DEADLINES
- All travel expenses must be submitted via the corporate expense portal within 30 DAYS of the trip's end date.
- Expenses submitted after 30 days will be permanently denied and become the employee's personal liability.

2. RECEIPT REQUIREMENTS (ITEMIZATION)
- Itemized Receipts Required: A standard credit card slip showing the total amount is NOT sufficient. The receipt must clearly list every item purchased.
- Meals: The receipt must show the specific food and beverages consumed. If alcohol was consumed during a non-client meal, the employee must manually deduct the alcohol cost from the reimbursement request before submission.

3. CURRENCY AND EXCHANGE RATES
- Corporate Card (Preferred): Employees must use the Veritas Corporate Credit Card for all bookings. The bank's exchange rate will apply automatically.
- Personal Card (Out-of-pocket): If a personal card is used for international expenses, the reimbursement will be calculated using the official European Central Bank (ECB) exchange rate corresponding to the exact date printed on the receipt.
