VERITAS CORPORATE - PREFERRED AIRLINE AGREEMENTS 2026

1. MANDATORY AIRLINE PARTNERS
To maximize our negotiated corporate discounts, employees MUST book flights with our preferred airline partners based on the destination region:

- Domestic Spain & Europe: Iberia (IB), Vueling (VY), Lufthansa (LH).
- Transatlantic & North America: British Airways (BA), Delta Airlines (DL), American Airlines (AA).
- Asia & Middle East: Emirates (EK), Qatar Airways (QR), Japan Airlines (JL).

2. BOOKING MANDATE AND PRICE TOLERANCE (15% RULE)
Employees are required to choose a preferred airline partner ONLY IF the flight cost is within a 15% tolerance of the lowest logical fare available on the market for that specific route and time.
- If the preferred airline's fare exceeds the cheapest alternative by more than 15%, the employee MUST book the cheaper alternative to save corporate funds.
- EXCEPTIONS: Ultra-low-cost carriers (e.g., Ryanair, easyJet, WizzAir) are EXCLUDED from this rule and should be avoided due to hidden baggage fees and reliability risks, unless no other direct flight exists.

3. LOYALTY PROGRAMS AND FREQUENT FLYER MILES
- Employees may accumulate personal frequent flyer miles (e.g., Oneworld, SkyTeam) on corporate flights.
- STRICT RULE: Personal loyalty preferences CANNOT override corporate policy. Choosing a more expensive flight outside the 15% tolerance rule solely to gain personal points is a direct policy violation and the difference will be deducted from the employee's payroll.
