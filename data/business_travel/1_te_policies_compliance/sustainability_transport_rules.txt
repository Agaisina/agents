VERITAS CORPORATE - SUSTAINABLE TRAVEL & GREEN POLICY 2026

1. SHORT-HAUL MANDATE (TRAIN VS. FLIGHT)
To reduce our corporate carbon footprint, flights are STRICTLY PROHIBITED for any route where a high-speed train alternative exists that takes less than 3.5 hours from city center to city center (e.g., Madrid-Barcelona, Paris-Lyon, London-Manchester).
- Employees must book trains in Standard/Turista class.
- Exceptions: If the train is fully booked or if the employee has a connecting international flight.
