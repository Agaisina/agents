VERITAS CORPORATE - ACCOMMODATION AND GROUND TRANSPORT

1. ACCOMMODATION SECURITY (DUTY OF CARE)
To ensure employee safety and corporate liability compliance, it is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN to stay in hostels, motels, or peer-to-peer rentals (e.g., Airbnb, Vrbo).
- Minimum Standard: 3-star corporate business hotel.
- Maximum Standard: 4-star business hotel. 5-star hotels are strictly prohibited unless hosting a Tier-1 client (requires CEO approval).

2. GROUND TRANSPORTATION
- Airport Transfers: Public transportation (train/subway) is mandatory if it is safe and efficient. Taxis or rideshares (Uber/Lyft) are only permitted if public transport takes more than 1.5 hours or for arrivals after 9:00 PM.
- Rental Cars: Only allowed if the destination has no public transport infrastructure. Must be booked through Hertz or Avis.
