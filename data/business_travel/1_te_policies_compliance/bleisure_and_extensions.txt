VERITAS CORPORATE - BLEISURE (BUSINESS + LEISURE) EXTENSIONS

1. FLIGHTS
The company will cover the cost of the return flight even if it is delayed to Sunday, PROVIDED that the flight cost is equal to or less than the original Friday flight.

2. ACCOMMODATION AND PER DIEM
The company will NEVER pay for hotels, meals, or transport for non-business days (e.g., Saturday and Sunday). All weekend expenses are 100% the responsibility of the employee.
