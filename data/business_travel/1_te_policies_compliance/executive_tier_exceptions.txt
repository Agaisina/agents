VERITAS CORPORATE - EXECUTIVE TIER EXCEPTIONS

The standard T&E policy applies to all standard employees and managers. However, Vice Presidents (VPs) and C-Level Executives (CEO, CFO, CTO) have the following exceptions:

1. ACCOMMODATION
- VPs and C-Level are authorized to stay in 5-star corporate hotels (e.g., Ritz-Carlton, Four Seasons).
- Nightly limit is extended to 400 EUR/night globally.

2. FLIGHTS
- VPs and C-Level may book Business Class for any flight exceeding 4 hours (instead of the standard 8 hours).
