VERITAS CORPORATE - NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

The following items will NOT be reimbursed under any circumstances:
1. Alcoholic beverages (unless explicitly part of an approved client dinner).
2. Room service and hotel minibar charges.
3. Traffic or parking fines.
4. Spa treatments, gym passes, or in-flight entertainment.
5. Personal hygiene items or clothing.
6. Cash advances without prior financial clearance.
