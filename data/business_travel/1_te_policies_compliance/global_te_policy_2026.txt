VERITAS CORPORATE - GLOBAL TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY 2026

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy applies to all corporate employees. Any expense exceeding these rules will be flagged as "Out of Policy" and automatically rejected by the Finance Department. The employee will be personally liable for non-compliant costs.

2. FLIGHT BOOKING RULES
- Advance Booking: All flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance to secure optimal pricing.
- Cabin Class: All flights under 8 hours of duration MUST be booked in Economy Class. Flights exceeding 8 hours may be booked in Premium Economy or Business Class, subject to direct VP approval.
- Upgrades: Employees may use personal frequent flyer miles for upgrades, but the company will only reimburse the Economy fare.
