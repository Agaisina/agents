VERITAS CORPORATE - CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT AND BUSINESS MEALS

1. DEFINITION AND LIMITS
When an employee hosts an external client for a business meal, standard Per Diem limits DO NOT apply. 
- Maximum allowance for Client Lunch/Dinner: 120 EUR per person (including taxes and tips).
- Alcohol: Moderate alcohol consumption is permitted ONLY during client dinners (maximum 2 drinks per person).

2. APPROVAL
Any client entertainment expense must include the name of the client and the company in the expense report.
